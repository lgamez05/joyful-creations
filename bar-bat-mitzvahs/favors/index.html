Personalized bar & bat mitzvah favors

Favors for a bar or bat mitzvah are personalized around the guest of honor and made by hand. This page does not list a catalog of finished products.

Questions

Personalized bar/bat mitzvah favor ideas?
Personalized favors for a bar or bat mitzvah are designed around the guest of honor and made by hand. This site does not list a catalog of finished products, because each order is custom and photographs of past pieces are not published yet. Tell us about the celebration and we will talk through pieces by email.
Is it handmade?
Yes. The pieces Joyful Creations LI makes are finished by hand. Design and making stay with the studio. We do not describe another producer's event, or another maker's work, as our own. Ask which parts of an order are handmade.
How far ahead should I order?
Most orders are planned about two weeks ahead; shorter timelines may be possible — ask us. That figure is a planning note, not a guaranteed turnaround, and it is not a promise of capacity. Tell us your date and we will say what we can consider.
