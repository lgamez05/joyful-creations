Questions

Which areas do you serve?
Joyful Creations LI serves celebrations in New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut, including Long Island and New York City. Serving an area is not a statement about delivery or on-site setup. Those details are not confirmed on this website. Tell us the city and state.
Is everything custom?
Yes. Everything is custom. Pieces are designed for your celebration rather than taken from a ready-made template. If something is outside the work this site describes, we will say so by email instead of adding it as a new offer.
Is it handmade?
Yes. The pieces Joyful Creations LI makes are finished by hand. Design and making stay with the studio. We do not describe another producer's event, or another maker's work, as our own. Ask which parts of an order are handmade.
