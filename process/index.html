Lead time

Most orders are planned about two weeks ahead; shorter timelines may be possible — ask us. That figure is a planning note, not a guaranteed turnaround, and it is not a promise of capacity. Tell us your date and we will say what we can consider.

Payment

Payment methods are not published on this website. This site does not take payment, deposits, or checkout. A change and cancellation policy is not published, so online payment is not offered. [PENDIENTE]

Questions

How far ahead should I order?
Most orders are planned about two weeks ahead; shorter timelines may be possible — ask us. That figure is a planning note, not a guaranteed turnaround, and it is not a promise of capacity. Tell us your date and we will say what we can consider.
Which payment methods do you accept?
This website does not take payment, deposits, or checkout. How an order is paid is confirmed with you by email after a quote, and the methods are not published here. We do not ask for tax identifiers or documents through these forms.
Do you deliver or set up?
Whether pieces are delivered, picked up, or set up on site is not confirmed on this website. Serving New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut means we take orders for celebrations there. Ask in your consultation and we will say what is possible. This is not a delivery promise.
