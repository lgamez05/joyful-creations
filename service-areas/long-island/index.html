Custom favors & stationery in Long Island

Joyful Creations LI serves celebrations in Long Island. This page does not list venues or local events. Delivery and on-site setup are not confirmed here.

Questions

Which areas do you serve?
Joyful Creations LI serves celebrations in New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut, including Long Island and New York City. Serving an area is not a statement about delivery or on-site setup. Those details are not confirmed on this website. Tell us the city and state.
Do you deliver or set up?
Whether pieces are delivered, picked up, or set up on site is not confirmed on this website. Serving New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut means we take orders for celebrations there. Ask in your consultation and we will say what is possible. This is not a delivery promise.
Do you publish prices?
No. Prices are not published on this website. Each order is priced by custom quote. There is no starting price and no budget field on the consultation form. Email us about your celebration and we will reply about the quote by email.
