Custom wedding invitations & suites

Invitation suites are designed and finished by hand. A lead time for invitations is not published on this page.

Questions

How far ahead should invitations be ordered?
A lead time for invitation suites is not published here. The planning note used for other orders is not used on this page. Share the date you need the suite, and we will reply by email about whether that date can be met. This is not a guaranteed schedule or a promise of capacity.
Is it handmade?
Yes. The pieces Joyful Creations LI makes are finished by hand. Design and making stay with the studio. We do not describe another producer's event, or another maker's work, as our own. Ask which parts of an order are handmade.
Do you publish prices?
No. Prices are not published on this website. Each order is priced by custom quote. There is no starting price and no budget field on the consultation form. Email us about your celebration and we will reply about the quote by email.
