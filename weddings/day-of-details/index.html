Questions

What should a seating chart include?
A seating chart should help guests find their table, using the details you and the studio agree on for that celebration. It may pair names with table numbers or show another arrangement you choose. Guest lists and venue names are not published on this website. The chart is paper we design and make by hand.
Do you do seating charts?
Yes. Seating charts are part of the day-of paper we design and make by hand, along with menus, escort cards, table numbers, and signage. What a chart includes is planned around your celebration. We do not publish guest names or venue names here.
Is it handmade?
Yes. The pieces Joyful Creations LI makes are finished by hand. Design and making stay with the studio. We do not describe another producer's event, or another maker's work, as our own. Ask which parts of an order are handmade.
