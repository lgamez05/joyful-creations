Questions

How far ahead should I order?
Most orders are planned about two weeks ahead; shorter timelines may be possible — ask us. That figure is a planning note, not a guaranteed turnaround, and it is not a promise of capacity. Tell us your date and we will say what we can consider.
Is everything custom?
Yes. Everything is custom. Pieces are designed for your celebration rather than taken from a ready-made template. If something is outside the work this site describes, we will say so by email instead of adding it as a new offer.
Do you do seating charts?
Yes. Seating charts are part of the day-of paper we design and make by hand, along with menus, escort cards, table numbers, and signage. What a chart includes is planned around your celebration. We do not publish guest names or venue names here.
